Fonfody

Explainable fund-risk early-warning platform
  • Turning a thesis algorithm (regime layer, L1–L5 signals, combined score) into a production-grade audit-prioritisation platform that joins Türkiye's BDDK/TCMB banking data with SPK/TEFAS fund data, scored by days of earlier warning.
